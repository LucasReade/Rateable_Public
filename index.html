Features

Everything you need to own your rating.

Eight capabilities, built around one idea: the person who sets the price should be the person who can change it, see how it was built, and prove it later.

Visual rating blocks

Fifteen composable blocks — Add, Subtract, Multiply, Divide, Lookup, If, And, Or, Not, Equal, MoreThan, LessThan, Round, Validation, Refer — ordered into an auditable waterfall. No code required.

Versions & effective dating

Every rate change is a version. Draft, publish, forward-date, and take a rate offline when a product leaves the market. Selection is automatic by quote date.

Live rate tables

Upload lookup tables, then edit rows inline as territory codes, fees and risk factors change — without touching the logic that reads them.

Eligibility & referrals

Validation blocks reject what doesn't fit; a Refer block hands borderline risks to an underwriter with a recorded reason — a standard insurance outcome, built in.

A fast, authenticated API

Quote via POST /quotes with an API key. The engine targets a sub-100ms average so quotes feel instant in your customer's journey.

Compliance-safe keys

API keys are hashed at rest with SHA-256, scoped to specific providers, revocable, and can carry an expiry — which is what makes 90-day rotation practical.

Quote-run visibility

Quotes by day, success versus engine errors, and referral counts per product and provider. No raw payloads stored, so no personal data in your logs.

Teams & invites

Invite team members into your organisation, hand out roles, and let everyone work against the same versioned truth instead of emailing spreadsheets around.

Security & compliance

The questions your procurement team will ask.

A rating engine holds your rate logic and sits in your customer journey, so these come up early. Here is what is true today.

Aggregates only

Quote payloads are not persisted. The engine keeps aggregates, so personal data never lands in your logs or ours.

Scoped, revocable keys

Keys are hashed at rest, scoped per provider, and can be revoked or expired without touching your other integrations.

Versioned and effective-dated

Every published rate carries a version number and an effective date, so a quote from six months ago can be traced to the logic that priced it.

Recorded referral reasons

When a risk is referred, the reason travels with it — so the decision is reviewable, not just the outcome.

Being straight with you: we are not yet independently certified, and we would rather say so here than badge it. Infrastructure detail, data residency, the DPA and our certification roadmap all get covered properly in the walkthrough.

Integration

One endpoint. Your integrators will be fine.

Quoting is a single authenticated call. Send a payload, get back a rated panel with the version number that produced it.

  • One endpoint POST /quotes. No SDK required — it is a JSON request and a JSON response.
  • Header auth Keys travel in X-Api-Key, scoped to a provider so you can separate environments and partners.
  • Automatic rate selection Send quoteStartDate; the engine picks the published version that was effective on that date.
  • Every quote is traceable Each returned quote carries its versionNumber, so a priced panel ties back to published logic.

Request

curl 
# Rating a quote: one request, one rated panel back
curl -X POST https://YOUR-API-HOST/quotes \
  -H "X-Api-Key: rt-demo-..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "payload": {
      "trade_type": "Electrician",
      "annual_turnover_gbp": 185000,
      "years_trading": 6,
      "claims_last_5yrs": 0
    },
    "quoteStartDate": "2026-09-10T09:00:00Z"
  }'

Good questions

Questions brokers ask us first.

How much does Rateable cost?

Every organisation starts on Standard at £249 per month — 1 product, 3 users and 50,000 quotes included. Beyond that it's £95 per month per extra product, £40 per month per block of five extra users, and a progressive per-quote rate that gets cheaper as volume grows. Past 14 products, 29 users or 2M quotes we scope a Custom plan. The calculator above shows your bill at any size.

Where is our data stored?

Quote payloads aren't persisted — the engine stores aggregates only, so no personal data sits in our logs. For infrastructure, data residency and the DPA, ask in your walkthrough and we'll share the details. We've also set out what we do and don't have covered in Security & compliance.

Can we import our existing rate tables?

Not yet — it's something we're working toward. In your walkthrough we'll scope an import for your panel and give you an honest timeline rather than a vague one.

Can we leave and take our logic with us?

Always. You own your products and rate logic. Export tooling is on the roadmap — tell us you need it and we'll prioritise it for your account.

Do we need developers?

No. Rating logic is built with visual blocks. The API is there for your integrators when you're ready to quote from your own portal.
